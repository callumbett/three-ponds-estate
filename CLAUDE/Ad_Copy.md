## Notes on the ChatGPT draft

Good strategic bones — the "invisible luxury" framing, the event roadmap, the seasonal angles are all sound. The copy itself needs work to match Three Ponds Estate's voice:

- "Winter Lull? Escape to Three Ponds" — self-deprecating, breaks brand register
- "Seamless self check-in · Total privacy" — clinical. Say "your pod is ready when you are"
- "Cozy up" / "marshmallows" — too cute, not refined
- "Reserve your pod now" / "Book your winter retreat today" — generic hotel CTAs
- ChatGPT said Google headlines can be "under 40 characters" — **incorrect**. Google Ads headlines are **30 characters max**. Descriptions are 90 characters max. Copy below is written to the correct specs.

## Master prompt (for future Claude / ChatGPT sessions)

Use this block whenever you need fresh copy. It contains everything needed to stay on-brand.

```
You are writing ad copy for Three Ponds Estate — a boutique accommodation 
property in Temora NSW, Australia.

PROPERTY:
- Three Scandinavian-inspired barn-style pods (The Ophir, The Felix, The Uphaz)
- 4.4-hectare property on quiet Riverina land
- 500m from Temora Aviation Museum, walking distance to Lake Centenary
- Gold Rush Ballooning launches directly across the road (canola season)
- Shared fire pit by the water, private decks, private parking
- Self check-in — no front desk, your pod is ready when you arrive. Host reachable if needed.
- From AU$230/night. No check-out on Saturdays.
- Reviews: 5.0 Google (114), 4.97 Airbnb (140), 9.6 Booking.com (130)

BRAND VOICE:
- Warm, refined, unhurried. Written as if the owner, Gillian, is speaking.
- Specific and sensory — name the actual things (the fire, the eastern deck, 
  the wide sky, the still morning)
- Never: "seamless", "cozy up", "total privacy", "don't miss out", "escape 
  the hustle", generic luxury hotel language
- Say instead: "your pod is ready when you are", "no agenda", "yours entirely",
  "the fire is already lit"
- The self check-in is a feature of freedom, not a process — frame it as 
  "no front desk, no schedule" rather than "seamless check-in"
- Master tagline: "The pace changes at the gate."

CAMPAIGN FOCUS: [INSERT — e.g. "Winter: fire by the water, cold mornings, 
warm pods, no schedule" OR "Canola season: balloons at sunrise, Lake Centenary 
Canola Walk walking distance" OR "Aviation Museum September Showcase: 500m from 
the warbirds"]

DELIVERABLES:
- 8 Google Ads headlines (30 characters MAX each — strict limit)
- 3 Google Ads descriptions (90 characters MAX each — strict limit)
- 3 Meta/Facebook primary text variants (150–250 words each, in Gil's voice)
- 1 Instagram caption (short, evocative, 3–5 lines of copy + hashtags)
- CTA button text for each Meta variant

Do not use exclamation marks. Do not write in listicle style. 
Write like a human who loves this place.
```
