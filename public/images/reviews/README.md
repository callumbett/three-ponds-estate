# Platform logos for the Reviews section

Drop the official brand logo files here, one per platform, with the
exact filenames listed below. They'll appear above the rating in each
review card on the homepage.

## Files expected

| Filename | Used by |
|---|---|
| `google.svg` (or `google.png`) | Google card |
| `airbnb.svg` (or `airbnb.png`) | Airbnb card |
| `booking.svg` (or `booking.png`) | Booking.com card |

## Specs

- **Format:** SVG strongly preferred (scales perfectly at any size, dark
  or light backgrounds). PNG with transparent background also works.
- **Aspect:** Each logo is rendered at a fixed height (~22px) — width
  scales automatically. Wide horizontal logos (like the Airbnb wordmark)
  work fine.
- **Colour:** Choose a version of the logo that reads cleanly on a
  parchment / cream background. The site is light, not dark.

## Where to find the official logos

- **Google** — search "Google brand resources" or just "Google logo SVG".
- **Airbnb** — `https://news.airbnb.com/about-us/` has brand assets at
  the bottom; or grab the wordmark + the "Bélo" symbol.
- **Booking.com** — their press kit at
  `https://news.booking.com/about-bookingcom/` typically has logos.

## Once uploaded

Tell Claude **"I've uploaded the platform logos"** with the filenames
you used and they'll be wired into each card.
