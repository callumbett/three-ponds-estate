# Story / heritage imagery

Photography for the homepage "Our Story" block AND the dedicated `/story` page.

## Files expected (exact filenames)

| Filename | Where it appears | Suggested subject |
|---|---|---|
| `homepage-tall.jpg` | Homepage "Our Story" block (4:5 portrait crop) | Fire pit at dusk, OR the open paddock with the pods just visible — something atmospheric, not strictly architectural |
| `story-page-wide.jpg` | Editorial image on `/story` (16:9 landscape) | A wide establishing shot of the country at golden hour — long horizon, low sun, no people |

If you only have one image and want to use it for both, name it `landscape.jpg` and tell Claude "use it for both" — it'll be cropped to fit each context.

## Specs

- Format: JPG
- Long edge: ~2800 px (the `/story` editorial shot is rendered large)
- Quality: 80–85 %
- Colour profile: sRGB

## Once uploaded

Say to Claude: **"I've uploaded the story images."**
